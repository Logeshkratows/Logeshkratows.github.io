Questions

Microgreens, simplified.

What are microgreens?

Microgreens are young edible plants harvested after the seedling stage. Different varieties offer different flavours, colours and textures.

How should I use them?

Add them just before serving to salads, sandwiches, wraps, bowls, breakfast dishes and plated restaurant meals.

Can restaurants buy in bulk?

Yes. A production schedule can be built around recurring weekly demand, crop variety and required pack weights.

Is the calculator a profit guarantee?

No. It is a planning model. Actual yield, wastage, labour, utilities, packaging, delivery, taxes and selling price should be measured from your operation.
